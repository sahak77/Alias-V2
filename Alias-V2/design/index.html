How to play
The goal
One player describes the on-screen word without saying it. Teammates shout guesses. Mark each word and race the timer — most points wins.
Marking words
✓
Correct — they guessed it · +1
↪
Skip — pass to the next word · 0 / −1
⚑
Foul — you said the word · −1
Game modes
Time Score
Each team plays a set number of rounds. Highest total wins.
Max Score
First to the target score wins. Finish the rotation for fairness.
Describe modes
DescribeTabooOne wordCharades
Ending & ties
Ends when all rounds are played (Time) or the target is hit (Max). Level scores show a draw — or play a sudden-death round.
